Fitness Science

Trial: two short strength sessions a week match three longer ones

A 12-week randomized trial of 120 adults found that two 30-minute full-body sessions a week produced the same strength gains as three 45-minute sessions. The authors note the sample was young and untrained, so the result may not carry over to experienced lifters. doi.org/10.0000/example.2026.001

Takeaway: consistency beat volume in this group. One trial is not a consensus.
